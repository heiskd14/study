ICT 305 — Data Communication System and Network
Time: 2½ Hours | Course Unit: 3 Units
Instruction: Answer Question ONE and any other THREE (3) Questions

Question 1
(a) A bank is designing a secure data communication network. Recommend suitable communication channels and data flow modes, justifying your choices.
(b) A telecommunications company plans to upgrade its network to support high-speed internet services. Explain how bandwidth, transmission impairments, and transmission type influence the design decision.
(c) Discuss transmission impairments and their effects on signal quality.
(d) Explain the OSI model and compare it with the TCP/IP model.

Question 2
(a) Explain the concept of data flow. (4 marks)
(b) Explain the three modes of data flow (simplex, half-duplex, full-duplex) with examples. (15 marks)
(c) Differentiate between analog transmission and digital transmission. (6 marks)

Question 3
(a) Describe Pulse Code Modulation (PCM) and explain its stages. (15 marks)
(b) Compare line coding techniques and explain their importance in data communication. (15 marks)
(c) Define guided and unguided transmission media. (4 marks)

Question 4
(a) Define encapsulation in networking. (3 marks)
(b) What is MAC addressing? (3 marks)
(c) State two differences between TCP and UDP. (4 marks)

Question 5
(a) What is a port number? (2 marks)
(b) Define QoS. (3 marks)
(c) A video streaming service requires low latency and high reliability. Which transport protocol should be used and why? (10 marks)

Question 6
(a) Given IP address 192.168.10.0/24, create four equal subnets and state their ranges. (10 marks)
(b) A video streaming service requires low latency and high reliability. Which transport protocol should be used and why? (10 marks)
(c) Describe layered architecture and the process of encapsulation with examples. (15 marks)
