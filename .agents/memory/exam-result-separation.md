---
name: Exam result separation
description: The product rule for storing and presenting student exam results.
---

Every student's exam result must remain a separate record; do not combine different students' scores or merge individual attempts into a shared result.

**Why:** The user said every person has different results and asked that they not be placed together.

**How to apply:** Admin result views and future exports should keep a distinct result row per exam attempt with the student's name and email.
